## FAQ

**Can I use a promo code on Sale items?** Generally no. Sitewide codes apply to full-price items, and the tiered event codes exclude markdowns plus a rotating brand list. Check the terms shown when you apply the code.

**Do promo codes and cashback stack?** Yes — that's the whole point of using a portal. Apply the code at Shopbop's checkout; the cashback tracks separately based on your final paid amount.

**How long do Shopbop returns take?** You have 30 days from delivery to return unworn items with tags attached, in original packaging. Shoes must go back in their original box with no shipping labels stuck to it. Final-sale items (the 50%-off-and-deeper tier) can't be returned, and original shipping fees aren't refunded.

**Is there a student discount?** No standing student, military or medical-worker discount program exists per current listings — the App and email first-order offers are the substitute.

**Why do coupon sites show codes that don't work?** Two reasons: Shopbop only runs codes during events (so most of the year there's nothing live), and aggregator pages often keep expired codes listed for months. The App offer and cashback don't have this problem because they're tied to your account, not a code string.
